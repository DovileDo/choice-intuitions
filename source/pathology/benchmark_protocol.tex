\subsection{Benchmark Protocol}
\label{sec:benchmark}

To compare practitioners' intuitions against empirical outcomes, we fine-tuned a ResNet-50~\cite{he2016} pretrained on each of the three candidate source datasets on the same target tasks used in the case studies. For each source--target combination, approximately 250 images were sampled for training and validation (80/20 split), with the remaining data reserved for testing. All splits were patient-disjoint and controlled by a single data seed.

The fine-tuning pipeline consisted of two phases. In the first phase, we searched for hyperparameters using Bayesian optimisation (Optuna~\cite{akiba2019}, 60 trials) over five data seeds; the configuration with the highest mean validation macro AUC across seeds was selected. In the second phase, we trained models with the selected hyperparameters on five held-out data seeds and evaluated them on the corresponding test sets. The search and evaluation seeds were fully disjoint (0--4 vs.\ 5--9), so that the hyperparameters were never selected on the same data used to report final results. We report mean and standard deviation of balanced accuracy, macro-averaged F1, and macro-averaged AUC across the five evaluation seeds. The full list of searched and fixed hyperparameters is provided in Appendix~\ref{app:hparams}.

For CS-tissue, training patches were sampled from NCT-CRC-HE-100K~\cite{kather2019} (250 per class) and the test set consisted of the remaining 4,930 patches from CRC-VAL-HE-7K; patient disjointness is guaranteed by construction, as the two sets originate from different institutions (NCT/UMM and TCGA, respectively). For CS-xray, we combined the official CheXpert~\cite{irvin2019} validation and test sets, retaining only images with at least one positive label among the eight pathologies with at least 100 positive examples (834 images, 662 patients), and performed patient-level splits to assign training and test partitions. Training used multi-class cross-entropy loss for CS-tissue and binary cross-entropy for CS-xray.
