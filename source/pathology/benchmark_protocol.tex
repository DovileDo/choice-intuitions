\subsection{Benchmark Protocol}
\label{sec:benchmark}

To compare practitioners' intuitions against empirical outcomes, we fine-tuned a ResNet-50~\cite{he2016} pretrained on each of the three candidate source datasets on the same target tasks used in the case studies. For CS-tissue, we sampled 250 patches per class from NCT-CRC-HE-100K~\cite{kather2019} for training and validation (80/20 stratified split), and used CRC-VAL-HE-7K with 250 patches per class held out as the test set (4,930 patches); patient disjointness is guaranteed by construction, as the two sets originate from different institutions (NCT/UMM and TCGA, respectively). For CS-xray, we combined the official CheXpert~\cite{irvin2019} validation and test sets, retaining only images with at least one positive label among the eight pathologies with at least 100 positive examples (834 images, 662 patients), and performed patient-level splits so that approximately 250 images were used for training and validation (80/20 split) and the remaining patients formed the test set. In both case studies, the training sample and test partition were controlled by a single data seed, and training used cross-entropy loss (multi-class for CS-tissue, binary for CS-xray).

The fine-tuning pipeline consisted of two phases. In the first phase, we searched for hyperparameters using Bayesian optimisation (Optuna~\cite{akiba2019}, 60 trials) over five data seeds; the configuration with the highest mean validation macro AUC across seeds was selected. In the second phase, we trained models with the selected hyperparameters on five held-out data seeds and evaluated them on the corresponding test sets. The search and evaluation seeds were fully disjoint (0--4 vs.\ 5--9), so that the hyperparameters were never selected on the same data used to report final results. We report balanced accuracy, macro-averaged F1, and macro-averaged AUC for CS-tissue, and macro-averaged AUC and per-pathology AUC for CS-xray, all as mean and standard deviation across the five evaluation seeds. The full list of searched and fixed hyperparameters is provided in Appendix~\ref{app:hparams}.
