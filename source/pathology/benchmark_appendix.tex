\section{Hyperparameter Details}
\label{app:hparams}

Table~\ref{tab:searched_hparams} lists the hyperparameters optimised via Bayesian search, and Table~\ref{tab:fixed_hparams} lists those held fixed across all experiments. Stain augmentation was searched only for CS-tissue (eight searched hyperparameters total, seven for CS-xray). Data augmentation was geometric in both case studies: random flips, rotations, and scaling. For CS-tissue, augmentation consisted of horizontal and vertical flips, random $90\degree$ rotations, and random scaling ($0.9$--$1.1\times$); because both NCT-CRC-HE-100K and CRC-VAL-HE-7K are Macenko-normalised~\cite{macenko2009}, we did not apply colour augmentation by default but included light HED-space stain perturbation~\cite{tellez2019} as a searched boolean. For CS-xray, images were resized directly to $224 \times 224$, with horizontal flips and random affine transformations ($\pm 10\degree$ rotation, $0.9$--$1.1\times$ scale, $\pm 5\%$ translation).
