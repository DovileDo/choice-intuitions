\section{Hyperparameter Details}
\label{app:hparams}

Table~\ref{tab:searched_hparams} lists the hyperparameters optimised via Bayesian search, and Table~\ref{tab:fixed_hparams} lists those held fixed across all experiments. Data augmentation consisted of random horizontal and vertical flips, random $90\degree$ rotations, and random scaling ($0.9$--$1.1\times$). Because both NCT-CRC-HE-100K and CRC-VAL-HE-7K are Macenko-normalised~\cite{macenko2009}, we did not apply colour augmentation by default; instead, we included light HED-space stain perturbation~\cite{tellez2019} as a searched boolean, letting the optimisation decide whether it improves performance given the cross-institutional gap between training (NCT/UMM) and test (TCGA) slides.
